The third edition of the Summer School on Cooperative Interacting Vehicles took place at Lake Tahoe, California, from July 31st to August 3rd, 2022. It gathered Ph.D. students and young professionals from universities and research institutions for tutorials, keynotes, poster presentations, and interactive workshop sessions on:

* Cooperative Perception
* Cooperative Motion Planning
* Implicit & Explicit Interaction
